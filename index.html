    Rules

    Everyone picked a final three and a winner. Final threes can overlap, but every winner pick is different.

    Champion

    🔥

    Points

    1 point for each of your three picks who makes the final three. Points decide 2nd place and below.

    ⚖️

    Tiebreaker

    Tied on points? Whoever's winner pick lasted longer in the game ranks higher.

    🗳️

    No one picked the winner?

    Most points becomes champion, using the same tiebreaker.
